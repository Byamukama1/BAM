Instructions

  1. Click USE EVERSEND TO PAY.
  2. Enter the amount you want to deposit.
  3. Fill in your names.
  4. Add a note e.g. Recharge BAM.
  5. Click Continue.
  6. Enter the same number you selected earlier (WhatsApp number to receive the OTP).
  7. Copy the OTP from WhatsApp and paste it.
  8. Click Make transaction and enter your PIN.
  9. After PIN, take a screenshot of the MTN/Airtel payment message.
  10. Upload the screenshot above and click Successful.
