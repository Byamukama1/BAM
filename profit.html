How the 100-Day Ad-Watching Plan Works

1) What you actually do each day

You complete short advert-watching tasks inside the BAM app. Most adverts are videos hosted on approved platforms (for example, YouTube). To count as a completed task, you:

  • Open the advert from your task list.
  • Watch for at least the minimum time shown on the Claim button (e.g., 10 seconds).
  • Tap Claim in the app to record your view and receive the reward. You have to click claim reward after watching.
If you close the video early or don’t press Claim reward, the system cannot reward that task.

2) How your earnings are calculated

Each level has a fixed number of daily tasks and a fixed amount paid per task. Your Daily Income is simply:

Daily Income = Tasks per day × Pay per task

When you complete your daily tasks for 100 days (three months plus 10 free days), you reach the 100-Day Total shown in the table.

3) Wallet, withdrawals, and missed days

  • Every successful claim is added to your in-app commission wallet.
  • You can withdraw on approved withdrawal days to mobile money or bank.
  • If you skip a day, you simply miss that day’s income; your level remains active.

4) Break-even and profit (simple idea)

Your deposit activates a level. The break-even day is when your total earnings equal the deposit. After that day, everything you earn is pure profit for the rest of the 100-day cycle.

5) Worked Example — Level B4

Start: Deposit UGX 625,000 to unlock B4.

Daily work: Complete 28 tasks, each paying UGX 735.

Daily income: 28 × 735 = UGX 20,580.

Break-even: 625,000 ÷ 20,580 ≈ 31 days (about one month).

100-day total: 20,580 × 100 = UGX 2,058,000.

Clear profit after 100 days: 2,058,000 − 625,000 = UGX 1,433,000.

Why B4 is popular: reasonable deposit, steady daily income, and a quick break-even.

6) Worked Example — Level B8

Start: Deposit UGX 30,000,000.

Daily work: Complete 175 tasks, each paying UGX 6,500.

Daily income: 175 × 6,500 = UGX 1,137,500.

Break-even: 30,000,000 ÷ 1,137,500 ≈ 27 days.

100-day total: 1,137,500 × 100 = UGX 113,750,000.

Clear profit after 100 days: 113,750,000 − 30,000,000 = UGX 83,750,000.

Best for disciplined users or teams who can complete many tasks every day without missing.

7) Quality & compliance rules

  • Always watch within the app and claim from the in-app button—external or repeated tabs won’t count.
  • Keep the video visible for the full countdown. Muting is allowed.

8) Tips for consistent earnings

  • Set a daily time to finish tasks so you never forget.
  • Stable internet reduces buffering and failed claims.
  • Choose the right level—only pick a level whose daily task count you can comfortably complete.
  • Track your break-even—after that, it’s all profit to day 100.
