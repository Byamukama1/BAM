How the 100-Day Ad-Watching Plan Works

1) What you actually do each day

You complete short advert-watching tasks in the BAM app. Watch videos, then tap Claim to earn rewards.

  • Open the advert from your task list.
  • Watch at least the required time (e.g., 10 seconds).
  • Tap Claim to record your view and get paid.
If you don’t press Claim, you won’t be rewarded.

2) How your earnings are calculated

Each level has fixed tasks and pay per task. The Daily Income is Tasks × Pay.

3) Wallet, withdrawals, and missed days

  • Each claim adds to your commission wallet.
  • You can withdraw on approved days.
  • Skipping a day just skips income; your level remains active.

4) Break-even and profit (simple idea)

Your deposit activates a level. The break-even day is when your total earnings equal your deposit. After that, you earn pure profit until day 100.
