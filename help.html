1. How to Deposit to your Main Wallet

Follow the steps below depending on your country.

Method: MTN / Airtel Money
Receiver: 0791884275
  1. Dial *165# (MTN) or *185# (Airtel).
  2. Choose Send Money.
  3. Enter number: 07650397.
  4. Enter the amount you want to send.
  5. Add a note (optional): Am paying.
  6. Enter your PIN to confirm.
  7. Copy the confirmation message (SMS).
  8. Return to BAM, paste the message and click Successful.
⚠️ Only click Successful after sending money successfully.
Method: Eversend Payment Link
Platform: https://eversend.me/collections
  1. Click or visit the Eversend Payment Page.
  2. Enter the amount shown on your deposit page.
  3. Use your registered BAM name and phone number.
  4. Complete payment securely using your country’s mobile wallet or card.
  5. After payment, wait for approval — admin will verify and add funds.
✅ No need to paste any message. Admin verifies Eversend payments automatically.

2. How Tasks Work

Tasks are adverts (mostly YouTube videos). You earn after watching and claiming.

  1. Open Tasks page.
  2. Tap a task → video plays.
  3. Tap Claim (10s) to start countdown.
  4. After 10 seconds, tap Claim Reward → reward is added.

3. Quick Help

  • Deposit not showing? Ensure you used the correct number or payment link.
  • “Already claimed” means you completed that task today.
