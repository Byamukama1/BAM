1. How to Deposit (Eversend)

These steps are exactly what you’ll see on the Eversend Payment page.

Method: Eversend
Reference: Recharge BAM
  1. Tap the yellow button TO USE EVERSEND TO PAY.
  2. Enter the amount and your names.
  3. Add a note: Recharge BAM.
  4. Use the same phone number to get OTP on WhatsApp.
  5. Enter OTP, then your PIN.
  6. Copy the message of the MTN/Airtel/GT Bank message.
  7. Back in BAM, paste the message → press Successful.
⚠️ Don’t click Successful before payment is finished (PIN entered) .

2. How Tasks Work

Tasks are adverts (mostly YouTube videos). You earn after watching and claiming.

  1. Open Tasks page.
  2. Tap a task → video plays.
  3. Tap Claim (10s) to start countdown.
  4. After 10 seconds, tap Claim Reward → reward is added.
Rewards go into your Commission Wallet. Each advert: once per day.
