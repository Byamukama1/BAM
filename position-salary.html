Position Monthly Salary (UGX)

Senior Team leader 1,050,000
Regional supervisor 4,300,000

Requirements

  • Adverts collector — 20 direct verified users & 10 referrals who paid for adverts.
  • Intern guider — 40 direct verified users & 20 referrals who paid for adverts.
  • Senior Team leader — 200 direct verified users & 50 referrals who paid for adverts.
  • Regional supervisor — 500 direct verified users & 100 referrals who paid for adverts.

How eligibility is checked

  • Direct verified users are people you referred (your code) whose account shows any level (B1–B9) with status verified.
  • Referrals who paid for adverts are unique users with at least one approved & paid advert in adverts that is tagged with your referrerA_uid.
  • We always award the highest role you qualify for. Clicking REQUEST SALARY creates a record in salaries with pending status for admin review.

Tip: If you need guidance on how to reach a salary level, contact your manager.
